Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Tel Khata ("the app") is developed and published by Appinion BD Ltd. ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account or sign-up.

The information you enter in the app, such as your vehicles, fuel fill-ups, expenses, engine oil records and settings, is stored only on your device.

How your information is used

Your data is used only inside the app to calculate mileage, show statistics and charts, and display engine oil reminders. It is not sent to us or to any third party.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, trade, rent or share your information with anyone.

Third-party services

The app does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking services.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Data retention and deletion

  • You can delete any entry or vehicle at any time inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently removes all of its data from your device.

Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by your device's own security.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date. If a future version of the app adds features such as cloud backup, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
